What we manage

Practical support, without another layer of complexity.

Choose the level of support that fits your team, infrastructure, and plans. The work stays grounded in the systems people use every day.

MANAGE / 01

Managed IT Services

End-to-end support for users, devices, servers, networks, security, backups, and everyday IT maintenance. We combine proactive monitoring and preventative work with responsive help when something needs attention.

Suits: Growing businesses that need experienced IT capability without carrying the full cost of an internal team.

MANAGE / 02

Microsoft 365 Support

Design, configuration, migration, user access, Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, Office apps, device management, and ongoing support. We also help protect the data and identities inside the tenant.

Suits: Teams moving to Microsoft 365 or businesses that want a partner to keep the platform secure and useful.

MANAGE / 03

Microsoft Azure Services

Cloud infrastructure, storage, application hosting, data management, identity protection, threat monitoring, analytics, and disaster recovery planning. Azure can scale with the workload instead of forcing the business into a fixed footprint.

Suits: Businesses modernising infrastructure, extending on-premises systems, or preparing applications for cloud delivery.

MANAGE / 04

Hosted Desktop

Secure, internet-hosted desktops give staff consistent access to applications and data from the office or remotely. The model can reduce hardware dependency, simplify support, and keep core applications available when they do not have a native cloud version.

Suits: Distributed teams, remote workers, and businesses that need controlled access to line-of-business software.

MANAGE / 05

Backup & Cloud Continuity

Cloud backup and recovery support helps protect business data and create a clearer route back from disruption. We assess the environment, use the platforms already available where sensible, and build practical recovery thinking into the service.

Suits: Businesses that cannot afford to treat recovery as an afterthought.

MANAGE / 06

Migration & Development

Move applications between data centres, on-premises infrastructure, private cloud, and public cloud environments. Where a new workflow is needed, low-code tools such as Microsoft Power Apps can make useful business applications faster to deliver.

Suits: Businesses changing platforms, adopting hybrid work, or replacing manual processes with focused applications.
