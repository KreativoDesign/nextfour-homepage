What we secure

Security that is practical enough to maintain.

Good security is not a single product. It is the combination of controls, data practices, clear ownership, and people who know what to do next.

SECURE / 01

Cybersecurity Strategy

Define a security approach around the organisation’s systems, people, data, and risk profile. We help select and implement controls including email and link scanning, firewalls, encryption, and security software.

Suits: Businesses that need a security plan tied to operations rather than a disconnected list of tools.

SECURE / 02

Endpoint & Network Protection

Protect the devices and network paths that connect staff to business systems. Open V’s technology partner stack includes ESET and FortiGate, supported by monitoring and practical security guidance.

Suits: Businesses managing multiple users, endpoints, sites, or remote access routes.

SECURE / 03

Microsoft 365 Security

Harden the Microsoft 365 environment, protect identities and data, and support safer use of Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, and Office applications. Security belongs in the tenant configuration and the daily habits around it.

Suits: Organisations using Microsoft 365 that want security built into support and deployment.

SECURE / 04

Data Protection & Compliance

Assess how data moves through systems and devices, then improve policies, platform controls, and staff awareness. The source framework covers GDPR, DPA, PECR, and FCA obligations; South African clients can also use the work to support POPIA-aligned practice.

Suits: Businesses that need to protect information and show how their data practices are managed.

SECURE / 05

Identity & Controlled Access

Apply a zero-trust mindset to access: make identity, device, permissions, and business need part of the decision. This reduces the assumption that everything inside the network should be trusted.

Suits: Hybrid, distributed, and growing teams with more users and systems to control.

SECURE / 06

Backup & Business Continuity

Protect data and plan the route back from ransomware, hardware failure, outages, or human error. Backup, recovery, and continuity thinking work best when they are assessed before the incident.

Suits: Businesses that need resilience to be tested and understood, not assumed.
