What we grow

Useful systems, connected to how the business runs.

Growth is not only a new website or a new tool. It is less friction in customer work, better operational information, and systems people can actually use.

GROW / 01

CRM & Business Management

Bitrix24-based CRM can bring together activity streams, group chat, calendars, workgroups, tasks, projects, time tracking, contact centre channels, and sales workflows. It can be hosted or on-premises, with implementation and staff training.

Suits: Businesses that need customer, sales, project, and collaboration work in one visible place.

GROW / 02

CCTV & AI Monitoring

Dahua WizMind systems combine high-definition cameras, secure network infrastructure, storage, monitoring, alerting, smart image identification, and advanced analytics. Use cases include people counting, restricted-area tracking, vehicle and parking analysis, privacy protection, and thermal detection.

Suits: Sites that need more than recorded footage, including proactive visibility and operational insight.

GROW / 03

Office Automation

HP office automation supports managed print and document workflows around the equipment, usage, and service model a business actually needs. The goal is a more controlled, supportable way to handle everyday documents.

Suits: Offices that want printing and document output to be predictable rather than an unmanaged cost.

GROW / 04

Time & Attendance

ERS biometric systems support attendance verification, hours calculation, exception notifications, access reporting, and online platform synchronisation. The ERS Watchdog app can also categorise computer activity and connect with facial or fingerprint verification.

Suits: Employers that need more accurate attendance records, access visibility, or workforce productivity information.

GROW / 05

Job Card Systems

A job card workflow gives service and operational teams a clearer way to capture work, assign responsibility, track progress, and retain a useful record of what happened. We will scope the workflow around the business rather than forcing a generic process.

Suits: Field, technical, maintenance, and service operations where work is currently tracked across messages or spreadsheets.

GROW / 06

Web Design & Hosting

Build a web presence that explains what the business does, supports enquiries, and stays maintainable after launch. Hosting and the surrounding technical setup should serve the site rather than become another mystery account.

Suits: Businesses replacing an outdated site or creating a clearer digital front door.

GROW / 07

Social Media

Set up and manage business profiles, plan and create content, schedule posts, review analytics, and use paid promotion where it has a clear purpose. The work starts with understanding the audience and the business goals.

Suits: Teams that need a consistent, credible presence without turning every post into an internal project.
