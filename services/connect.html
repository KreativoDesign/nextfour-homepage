What we connect

The infrastructure behind work that cannot buffer.

Connectivity is not just a line or a handset. It is the network, voice system, physical infrastructure, and monitoring that lets people do their jobs.

CONNECT / 01

VoIP & PABX Systems

Business voice systems with call routing, transfers, waiting, conferencing, video calling, and the flexibility to handle inbound and outbound traffic. The right design can improve voice quality and reduce telecom cost.

Suits: Businesses replacing analogue systems, scaling teams, or needing a more useful phone workflow.

CONNECT / 02

Call Centre Solutions

Bring calls, email, web chat, SMS, and social channels into a more consistent customer service operation. Routing and visibility help teams handle interactions and identify where service can improve.

Suits: Customer-facing teams that need to manage more conversations without losing context.

CONNECT / 03

Networking & Connectivity

Network design, installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and support across switches, routers, servers, operating systems, and applications. We can manage ISP relationships with providers such as Vodacom, MTN, FrogFoot, VOX, ECN, and MetroFibre.

Suits: Businesses where network performance and uptime directly affect the working day.

CONNECT / 04

Network Monitoring

Firewall, IP, LAN/WAN, performance, fault, downtime, and intrusion monitoring, with alerts and reporting that make network health visible. Iris can deliver cloud, on-premises, or hybrid monitoring for different business sizes.

Suits: Teams that want issues identified before users discover them through downtime.

CONNECT / 05

WiFi & Managed Wireless

Business wireless designed for coverage, performance, and security, with ongoing management rather than a collection of consumer access points. Open V has training and certification through network partners including Vodacom.

Suits: Offices, customer spaces, warehouses, and distributed sites with more demanding wireless needs.

CONNECT / 06

Structured Cabling

Cat 5 and Cat 6 installation, fibre installation and splicing, PABX, CCTV and biometric infrastructure, managed wireless installation, and microwave links. Work is planned for reliable transfer, maintainability, and safe installation.

Suits: New builds, office changes, outsourced infrastructure projects, and sites with unreliable physical networks.

CONNECT / 07

Failover Internet

A managed failover approach keeps the business connected when the primary service is interrupted. We assess the site and use the most suitable available ISP relationship instead of forcing every client into the same answer.

Suits: Businesses where a dead primary link quickly becomes lost productivity or lost revenue.
