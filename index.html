Straight Answers

Common questions, answered directly.

What does Open V Business Solutions do?

Open V Business Solutions is a managed IT provider delivering managed IT services, cybersecurity, business connectivity, VoIP, and cloud services to South African businesses since 2006.

Where is Open V Business Solutions based?

We are headquartered in Gqeberha and serve clients across South Africa, with national reach across major business centres and regional operations.

Is Open V Business Solutions POPIA compliant?

Yes. We align our data handling and client data protection practices with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What cybersecurity vendors does Open V partner with?

Our cybersecurity services are powered by ESET and FortiGate, alongside Microsoft 365 security and POPIA-aligned data protection.

What is Open V's response time commitment?

We operate on a 24-hour turnaround commitment, with clear ownership and accountability throughout the delivery team so issues move forward without being lost in a queue.

Who owns Open V Business Solutions?

Open V Business Solutions is owned and personally led by Siva Naidoo and Ravi Naidoo, who have run the business since 2006.
